About

Hi! I'm Sheeraz, a postdoctoral researcher in Prof. Nikolaus Correll's Physical AI and Robotics (PAIR) Initiative at the University of Notre Dame, working on dexterous robotic hands.

My research spans multimodal tactile sensing, soft and compliant robotics, underwater robotics, and learning-enabled perception — with a common thread of building real systems that work reliably outside controlled lab settings. I'm drawn to embodied intelligence: the idea that sensing, mechanics, and control are most powerful when treated as one unified system.

I completed my PhD in Industrial Engineering at Purdue University under Prof. Yu She (MARS Lab), and my M.Phil. at HKUST's Cheng Kar-Shun Robotics Institute. I'm always open to collaborations — feel free to reach out.
