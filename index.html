Sheeraz Athar

Postdoctoral ResearcherPhysical AI & Robotics Initiative · University of Notre Dame

I'm a postdoctoral researcher in the Physical AI & Robotics (PAIR) Initiative at the University of Notre Dame, working with Prof. Nikolaus Correll. Previously, I was a Ph.D. student in the MARS Lab at Purdue University with Prof. Yu She. Before that, I earned an M.Phil. from HKUST with Prof. Michael Yu Wang and a B.Tech. from Aligarh Muslim University. I work on how robots can use touch and physical contact to perform dexterous tasks that remain difficult to automate. During my Ph.D., I developed multimodal tactile sensors for robotic manipulation, tactile sensing for robot-assisted minimally invasive surgery (RAMIS), and a multimodal underwater robot that drifts, glides, and thrusts. My current work focuses on dexterous robotic hands and humanoid robots that bring physical AI to challenging industrial applications.

Research program

Robots are moving out of structured factories into hospitals, oceans, and homes, where they must handle contact, uncertainty, and harsh conditions. My work connects three layers: sensors that capture rich physical information, soft and dexterous mechanisms that act on it, and complete systems tested in the field.

  • 01

    Dexterous hands & grasping

    Hands and grippers that sense and adapt while they touch — from a visuo-tactile active palm to in-hand reorientation of syringes and millimeter-scale parts.VTAP Gripper · Notre Dame PAIR

  • 02

    Multimodal tactile sensing

    Fingertips that fuse geometry, force, vibration, and texture in one device.VisTac · VibTac · MuxGel

  • 03

    Soft & bio-inspired robotics

    Fiber-embedded and auxetic structures that deform safely and sense as they move.FibTac

  • 04

    Underwater robotics

    One robot that drifts, glides, and thrusts — built for long missions.Multimodal underwater robot

  • 05

    Surgical tactile sensing

    Millimeter-scale sensors that bring touch to minimally invasive surgery.TacScope · MVP-Tac
