Sheeraz AtharPostdoctoral Researcher · University of Notre Dame

I build robots
that can feel.

I work with Prof. Nikolaus Correll in Notre Dame's Physical AI & Robotics (PAIR) Initiative on dexterous robotic hands. I design tactile sensors, soft mechanisms, and the learning pipelines that let robots understand contact — and I test all of it on real hardware, from millimeter-scale surgical sensors to a full-size underwater robot.

Previously Ph.D. at Purdue with Prof. Yu She · M.Phil. at HKUST · B.E. at Aligarh Muslim University

Research

Most robots can see the world but can't feel it. My work closes that gap from both ends — sensors that capture rich contact information, and hands and mechanisms that can act on it.

Current focus
01

Dexterous hands & grasping

Hands and grippers that sense and adapt while they touch — from a visuo-tactile active palm to in-hand reorientation of syringes and millimeter-scale parts.

VTAP Gripper · IROS 2026 Best Paper Finalist · Notre Dame PAIR
02

Multimodal tactile sensing

Fingertips that fuse geometry, force, vibration, and texture in one device.

VisTac · VibTac · MuxGel
03

Soft & bio-inspired robotics

Fiber-embedded and auxetic structures that deform safely and sense as they move.

FibTac · npj Robotics 2026
