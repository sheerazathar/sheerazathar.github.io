Outreach

Robotics demonstrations, lab visits, and mentoring for pre-college, community-college, and first-year engineering students, run through Purdue's Minority Engineering Program and partner programs in 2024 and 2025.

September2025

Purdue FIRST Robotics

Demonstrations for FIRST Robotics students on how mechanical design, sensing, and programming come together in research robots.

July2025

MITE — Multiethnic Introduction to Engineering

Robotics demonstrations and mentoring for pre-college students, organized by Purdue's Minority Engineering Program.

June2025

Blue Sky Summer Scholars

Hosted Ivy Tech Community College students in the MARS Lab for robot demonstrations and a discussion of pathways into engineering research.

June2025

PREFACE Summer Program

Mentoring and an introduction to robotics for incoming first-year engineering students, many of them first-generation college students.

June2024

PREFACE Summer Program

Guided lab tours and interactive demonstrations for first-generation and underrepresented engineering students.

June2024

MITE — Multiethnic Introduction to Engineering

Robotics demonstrations, lab tours, and informal mentoring for pre-college students.
