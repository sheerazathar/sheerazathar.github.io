Broadening participation

Outreach

I treat outreach as an extension of teaching — creating early, welcoming entry points into robotics for students from diverse and historically underrepresented backgrounds. Robots are the best hook I know: once a student has held a sensor that can feel, engineering stops being abstract.

Purdue FIRST Robotics

Demonstrations for FIRST Robotics students showing how mechanical design, sensing, and programming come together in real research robots. FIRST ↗

July2025

MITE — Multiethnic Introduction to Engineering

Interactive robotics demonstrations and mentoring for pre-college students, connecting the math and physics they already know to robots they can touch. Run with Purdue's Minority Engineering Program.

Blue Sky Summer Scholars

Hosted Ivy Tech Community College students in the MARS Lab for robot demonstrations, perception-based manipulation, and an honest conversation about pathways into engineering research.

PREFACE Summer Program
