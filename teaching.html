Teaching & mentorship

Teaching

Six years of teaching robotics, design, and manufacturing at Purdue and HKUST — in the lab, at the bench, and alongside students building their first real systems.

Philosophy

Students learn engineering best when they build, test, question, and iterate.

Robotics is a hands-on discipline. Real understanding comes from watching an algorithm drive real hardware, seeing a sensor respond to the world, and diagnosing why a design choice changed how a system behaves.

I try to connect foundational theory to the messy realities of practice — noise, calibration drift, timing, tolerances, and failure modes — while building the judgment and communication skills students need for research and industry.

Above all, I work to make my classrooms and labs places where students from every background feel confident experimenting, asking questions, and getting things wrong on the way to getting them right.
