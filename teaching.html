Teaching & Mentoring

I have taught robotics, design, and manufacturing courses at Purdue University and HKUST since 2020, and have mentored undergraduate and graduate researchers in the MARS Lab.

Teaching philosophy

Robotics is learned by building. Students understand an algorithm when they watch it drive real hardware, and they understand a sensor when they see it respond to the world. My courses pair foundational theory with laboratory work on physical systems, so that noise, calibration, timing, and tolerances become part of the lesson rather than an afterthought.

I aim to develop the judgment and communication skills students need for research and industry, and to run classrooms and labs where students from every background can experiment, ask questions, and learn from failure.
