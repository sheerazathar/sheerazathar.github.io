Teaching Philosophy

Students learn engineering most effectively when they build, test, question, and iterate. Robotics is inherently hands-on — real understanding comes from watching algorithms drive real hardware, seeing sensors respond to the world, and diagnosing how design choices shape system behavior.

My goal is to connect foundational theory to the messy realities of engineering practice — noise, calibration drift, timing, tolerances, failure modes — while building the judgment and communication skills students need for research and industry. I work to create environments where students from every background feel confident experimenting and asking questions.
